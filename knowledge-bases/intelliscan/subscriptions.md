## Common Subscription Issues

### "Website limit reached"

**Symptom:** Cannot add new website, error about limit.

**Cause:** Plan limit exceeded.

**Fix:**
1. Check current plan limits (see above)
2. Remove unused websites from dashboard
3. Upgrade to higher plan for more websites

**How to Remove a Website:**
1. Go to Dashboard → Websites
2. Click on the website to remove
3. Select "Delete Website" option
4. Confirm deletion

**Note:** Deleting a website also deletes all associated reports.

---

### "Scheduled scans not running"

**Symptom:** Automatic scans not happening on schedule.

**Possible Causes:**
1. Using Free plan (manual only)
2. Website was recently added (first scan pending)
3. Technical issue with scheduler

**Verify Plan:**
1. Go to Settings → Subscription
2. Check current plan
3. Free plan = manual scans only

**Scan Schedule by Plan:**
| Plan | Schedule |
|------|----------|
| Free | Manual only |
| Starter | 1st of each month |
| Pro | 1st and 15th of each month |
| Agency | Every Monday |

**Escalate to L2 if:**
- Paid plan user and scans haven't run for 2+ cycles
- Website shows as "Active" but no recent scans

---

### "Cannot upgrade plan"

**Symptom:** Upgrade button not working or payment fails.

**Troubleshooting:**
1. **Check payment method:**
   - Valid credit card required
   - Ensure card not expired
   - Sufficient funds available

2. **Try different payment method:**
   - Different credit card
   - Contact bank if card declined

3. **Browser issues:**
   - Clear cache and cookies
   - Try incognito mode
   - Use different browser

**Escalate to L2 if:**
- Payment confirmed but plan not upgraded
- Charged but features not activated

---

### "Plan features not working after upgrade"

**Symptom:** Upgraded but still seeing old plan limits.

**Fix:**
1. Log out and log back in
2. Refresh browser (Ctrl+Shift+R)
3. Wait 5 minutes for sync
4. Check email for upgrade confirmation

**If Still Not Working:**
- Check payment confirmation email
- Verify charge on credit card statement
- Escalate to L2 with payment proof

---

### "Want to cancel subscription"

**Self-Service Cancellation:**
1. Go to Dashboard → Settings → Subscription
2. Click "Cancel Subscription"
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Access continues until end of billing period

**What Happens on Cancellation:**
- Immediate: Scheduled scans stop
- End of period: Account reverts to Free plan
- Data: Reports remain accessible as read-only
- Websites: Only 1 website retained (oldest)

**Refund Policy:**
- No refunds for partial months
- Cancel anytime, no penalty
- Contact support for billing issues

---

### "Billing date questions"

**How Billing Works:**
- Charged on signup date each month
- Example: Sign up March 15 → billed March 15, April 15, etc.
- Upgrades: Prorated for remaining days
- Downgrades: Take effect at next billing cycle

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Can I switch plans anytime?"
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated). Downgrades take effect at next billing cycle.

### "What happens to my data if I downgrade?"
Reports are retained (read-only). If website count exceeds new limit, only oldest website is kept.

### "Is there a free trial of paid features?"
The Free plan lets you try core functionality. No time-limited trial of paid tiers.

### "Do you offer annual billing?"
Not currently. Monthly billing only.

### "Can I get a refund?"
No refunds for used time. Contact support if you were charged incorrectly.
